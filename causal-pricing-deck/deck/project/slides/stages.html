Click 1, why LightGBM in the first stage - Many rating factors: learns shapes and interactions without us specifying them, unlike a GLM. - Categories and gaps: native categorical features (also many levels) and missing values. - Fast: both first-stage models are refitted once per fold. - Only needs to predict well: final-stage bias is a product of first-stage errors (convergence slide). Alternatives, if asked - GLM or Lasso: too rigid for many factors; errors don't shrink, bias stays. - Random forests, neural networks: possible, more tuning. LightGBM = pragmatic default.
