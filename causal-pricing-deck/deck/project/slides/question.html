Message: pricing asks a what-if (intervention); a lapse model answers "who lapses at which price" (prediction). - They differ because we set prices from the same factors that drive lapse. Builds - Click 1: tariff rises with age. - Click 2: older customers lapse less. - Click 3: the arrow we want, price → lapse (θ). Every other path runs through age: confounding. - Click 4: the toy world. Toy world (known truth, so every method can be graded) - Age 25–75, one rating factor (in practice: all rating factors). - Tariff: +2.5% per 10 years of age (+13% from 25 to 75). - Plus discounts and loadings, SD 2%, symmetric: the only "experiment" in the data. - Lapse = g(age) + θ·T, θ = 1: +1% price → +1.0 pp lapse for everybody. Average lapse 26%. - 200,000 policies; effect sizes exaggerated, mechanisms as in real data.
