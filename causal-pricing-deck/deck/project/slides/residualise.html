How Double ML works

Double ML removes what age predicts from price and lapse, then reads the effect

Partially linear model

X: age · g: age effect
V: free price variation

subtract what age predicts, on both sides →

lapse model, price model: what age predicts

1 · Predict lapse from age

Lapse model · first stage

2 · Predict price from age

Price model · first stage

3 · Regress residual on residual

Slope = price effect · final stage

First stages cross-fitted in 5 folds: each policy is predicted by models that never saw it

Message: one equation, three steps. The grey strip is the model; the three columns are how it is fitted. The model - Y = θ·T + g(X) + ε: price effect + age effect of any shape + noise. - T = m(X) + V, E[V | X] = 0: tariff + discounts and loadings. - ℓ(X) = E[Y | X] = θ·m(X) + g(X), so Y − ℓ(X) = θ·(T − m(X)) + ε. - Age effect gone: θ = slope of lapse residual on price residual (Frisch–Waugh–Lovell with ML). g is never fitted directly. Builds (aqua = lapse model, violet = price model, blue = Double ML effect) - Click 1: LightGBM predicts lapse from age, ℓ̂(X) (econml model_y); Ỹ = Y − ℓ̂(X). - Click 2: LightGBM predicts price from age, m̂(X) (model_t), recovers the tariff; T̃ estimates V. - Click 3: least squares of Ỹ on T̃ through the origin: θ̂ = 1.02, SE 0.049, 95% CI 0.92–1.11. Truth 1.00. - Dots are 20 bins of T̃; the regression uses all 200,000 policies. Key points - Same learner, same tuning as before: 0.82 with price as a feature, 1.02 when LightGBM only handles the age parts. - Information comes only from V. Pure tariff (no discounts, loadings, tests, tariff changes) → θ not identified, CI widens. If asked - "Would our GLM do the same?" Here yes (1.02): tariff is linear in age. With many factors a GLM needs hand-built interactions. - Cross-fitting: train on 4 folds, predict the 5th. In-sample fits memorise discounts and noise → bias. Here in-sample gives 1.018; it bites with many factors. - econml: LinearDML(model_y=..., model_t=..., cv=5).fit(Y, T, X=None, W=age); early stopping needs a small wrapper.
