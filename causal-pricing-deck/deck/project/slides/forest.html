Message: why the causal forest was our final stage: three properties, the result, then the deck takeaway. Builds - Click 1, effect differences: a normal tree splits where lapse differs; the causal forest splits where the price effect differs. That is the pricing question. - Click 2, honest trees: half of each tree's sample picks the splits, the other half estimates leaf effects. - A split that looks good by chance does not get to set its own effect. Main difference from the boosted final stage. - Click 3, uncertainty: honesty makes valid CIs possible (Wager and Athey, 2018; GRF, Athey, Tibshirani and Wager, 2019). Thin or noisy segments become visible. - Click 4, in our project: best of the three. More flexible than linear, more reliable than boosted. Part of the gain is likely honesty itself. - Click 5, takeaway: prediction models to predict; causal estimators, with explicit assumptions, to decide. If asked - econml: CausalForestDML with LightGBM as model_y and model_t, cross-fitted as before. - The assumptions from "What Double ML needs" still hold.
