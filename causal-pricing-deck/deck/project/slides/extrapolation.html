LightGBM on age and price gets the sign right, but freezes beyond the data

Where the data is: price vs age

At any age, observed prices sit close to the tariff; +20% moves every customer beyond them.

Lapse rate after a price change for all customers

Inside the range: right sign, slope too low (0.82 vs 1.0). Beyond it, LightGBM uses older customers’ lapse, then freezes.

Message: adding age fixes the sign locally, but the what-if we care about lies outside the data. Setup - LightGBM on (age, price), early stopping on a 20% hold-out (53 rounds): tuned for prediction. - What-if: multiply every price by (1 + x), keep age, average predicted lapse. Builds - Start: what matters is the price range given age, not the global range: within ≈ ±4% of tariff (±2 SD). Name: overlap / positivity. - Click 1: +20% → no observed 30-year-old pays that much. - Click 2: the truth. Ask: "What will LightGBM predict at +20%?" - Click 3: LightGBM. - Inside the range: right sign, slope 0.82 instead of 1.00 (regularisation mutes the weak signal; see convergence slide). - +8%: borrows older customers' response (31.7% predicted vs 34.2% true). - From ≈ +12%: freezes. 33% at +20% (truth 45%), 33% at +40% (truth 60%). Why flat - Trees are piecewise constant: beyond the highest price split (≈ +17% vs age-25 base) every prediction lands in the same leaf. - Lapse stops responding, so margin looks like free money (Decision slide). Honest note: every method extrapolates only through assumptions. Trees assume flat; Double ML assumes linear in log price.
